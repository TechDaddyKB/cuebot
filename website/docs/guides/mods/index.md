# Mods and your phone

This part is about getting help, and taking Cuebot with you. Your moderators can help with your stream from anywhere,
and the phone app puts your buttons, your stream's numbers and your mods' changes in your pocket.

## What's in here

- [The mod portal](mod-portal.md): let your mods run actions, change commands, upload sounds and more, from their own
  phone or browser.
- [The phone app](phone-app.md): your decks, your dashboard and your mods' changes on Android, and mod mode for
  helping someone else.
