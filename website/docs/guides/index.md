# Guides

Each guide explains one job, step by step, with a stream example. They are grouped by topic. New to Cuebot? Start at
the top and work down.

| Topic | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| [Getting started](start/index.md) | Install and update Cuebot, your first five minutes, moving from Streamer.bot, and a map of every page. |
| [Your platforms](platforms/index.md) | Sign in to Twitch, YouTube, Kick and Rumble, and keep personas apart with profiles. |
| [Actions and automation](actions/index.md) | Actions, triggers and the flowchart, variables, queues, timers, hotkeys, watchers and history. |
| [Chat and community](chat/index.md) | Chat commands, points, giveaways, the store, polls, quotes, credits, top spenders, users and groups. |
| [Overlays and alerts](overlays/index.md) | Put things on your stream: overlays, alerts and your own widgets. |
| [Speech, sound and music](sound/index.md) | Text-to-speech, voice control, now playing, Spotify, song and media requests. |
| [Stream apps and devices](apps/index.md) | OBS and other stream apps, the Stream Deck, controllers, lights and VTuber apps. |
| [Donations and services](services/index.md) | Tips, Ko-fi and other webhooks, the Discord bot, subathon timers and goals. |
| [Mods and your phone](mods/index.md) | The mod portal and the phone app. |
| [The AI assistant](ai/index.md) | Let an AI set Cuebot up for you, on your computer or with your own AI app. |
| [Code and connections](developers/index.md) | C# and Python code, and the servers other programs use to talk to Cuebot. |
| [Your data and help](help/index.md) | Your data, backups, Linux, troubleshooting and what's experimental. |
