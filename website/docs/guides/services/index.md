# Donations and services

This part is about tips, donations, web services and your Discord server, plus the subathon timers and goals that
those events feed. Most of it is under **Apps** in the left rail. Keys and tokens you type in are hidden on screen,
stored locked (encrypted) on your computer, and never written to the log.

## What's in here

- [Tips and donations](tips-and-donations.md): how tip sites work with Cuebot, and which one to pick.
  - [Streamlabs](streamlabs.md): donations, plus alert box control, wheel, jar, credits, media share, loyalty points
    and missed-donation back-fill with your own Streamlabs app.
  - [StreamElements](streamelements.md): tip moderation, going live, session data, the chatbot, store, contests,
    giveaways and song requests.
  - [TipeeeStream](tipeeestream.md), [Pally.gg](pally.md), [DonorDrive (Extra Life)](donordrive.md) and
    [TreatStream](treatstream.md).
- [Webhooks](webhooks.md): how webhook services reach Cuebot through a relay.
  - [Ko-fi](ko-fi.md): private supports, the Ko-fi goal, memberships and commissions.
  - [Patreon](patreon.md): monthly amounts and currency, upgrades, tiers, patron perks in chat, the API connection.
  - [Fourthwall](fourthwall.md): only paid donations and real purchases count, hidden names, memberships, stream
    gift draws from chat, discount codes, the products widget and catch-up with the Fourthwall API.
  - [Shopify](shopify.md): paid, cancelled and refunded orders, buyers' privacy, a merch counter and goal, stock
    alerts, the Webhook API version, and the products widget and catch-up with the Shopify API.
  - [Throne](throne.md): wishlist gifts.
  - [Custom webhooks](custom-webhooks.md): let any other program or website start your actions.
- [IFTTT](ifttt.md): send events to IFTTT so it can do something else, like a phone notification.
- Heart rate: [Pulsoid](pulsoid.md) and [HypeRate](hyperate.md).
- Viewer games: [StreamLoots](streamloots.md) (cards and chests) and [Crowd Control](crowd-control.md) (viewers
  change your game).
- [Discord bot](discord-bot.md): your own bot for "I'm live" posts, roles and slash commands.
- [Subathon timers](subathon-timers.md): a countdown that subs, bits and tips add time to.
- [Goals](goals.md): a ladder of steps that events add points to.
