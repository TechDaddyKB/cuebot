# Stream apps and devices

Cuebot can work with the other programs and gadgets on your desk: OBS, your Stream Deck, your VTuber app, lights,
mixers and controllers. Each connection gives you two things:

- **triggers**: the app or device can *start* your actions (a button press, a scene change);
- **sub-actions**: your actions can *control* the app or device (switch a scene, change a light).

Nothing connects by itself until you set it up, and most pages have a box to connect automatically next time.

## What's in here

- [Other stream apps](other-stream-apps.md): Streamlabs Desktop, Meld Studio, PolyPop, T.I.T.S., Lumia Stream, OBSBOT

- [Stream Deck](stream-deck.md): run actions from an Elgato Stream Deck.
- [Decks](decks.md): buttons on your phone or tablet, in a web browser.
- [Controllers and MIDI](controllers.md): MIDI pads, game controllers, hotkeys and your voice.
- [Lights, mixers and other apps](lights-and-mixers.md): Elgato lights, Wave Link, GoXLR, VTube Studio, Voicemod and
  more.
- [VTuber apps](vtuber/index.md): make your avatar react, in veadotube, VNyan, VRChat, Warudo and others.

## On Linux

- **Game controllers:** PlayStation and Switch pads need extra permission for rumble and lights. Install your
  distribution's `steam-devices` or `game-devices-udev` package.
- **Stream Deck:** see [Stream Deck on Linux](stream-deck.md#linux-the-permission-rule).
