# Your data and getting help

These pages are about keeping your setup safe and getting unstuck: where Cuebot keeps your things, backups, Linux
extras, start options, fixing problems and what is still being tested.

- [Your data and privacy](your-data.md): where Cuebot keeps your settings, how sign-ins are locked, and what is sent where.
- [Backups](backups.md): let Cuebot make backups by itself, and get one back.
- [Backups on Google Drive](google-drive-backups.md): keep copies of your backups on your own Google Drive.
- [Linux](linux.md): the few things that are different on Linux, like the tray, sound and devices.
- [Command-line options](command-line-options.md): extra words that change how Cuebot starts.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): when something does not happen, common problems, and how to ask for help.
- [What's experimental](experimental.md): the features not yet tried with the real app or service.
