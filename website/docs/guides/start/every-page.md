# Every page in Cuebot

This page is a map. It lists every page in Cuebot, in the order of the left rail, with one line about each and a link
to the guide that explains it. Each heading is the title of a rail section; the short name under its rail icon is in
brackets.

!!! tip "Finding a page fast"
    Press ++ctrl+k++ and type part of a page name, like `music`, then press ++enter++.

## The Chat page

**Chat > Chat** shows the chat of every platform you are signed in to, in one list.

1. Type in the box at the bottom and press ++enter++ (or click **Send**) to write in chat. The list next to it picks
   where it goes: one platform, or all connected ones.
2. Click a message to see what you can do with it: **Reply**, **Delete message**, **Timeout 1 min**, **Timeout 10
   min**, or ban the person (click once, then again to confirm).
3. In the send box, type `:` for emotes, or `/help` for the slash commands.
4. The chat options let you show emotes as pictures (also from 7TV, BTTV and FFZ), show pronouns, colour messages
   that mention your name or come from first-time chatters, and add **quick action** buttons that run an action.
5. The **Activity** tab lists follows, subs, raids, tips and other events.

Want the chat inside OBS? The [HTTP server](../developers/http-server.md) gives you a chat dock and an event dock.

## The Settings page

- **General**: **Show an icon in the system tray** (ticked at first) keeps Cuebot next to the clock. **Closing the window keeps
  Cuebot running in the tray** hides it instead of closing it. **Start minimized** starts it hidden. **Theme** picks
  light, dark (the first choice), or **System** (the same as your computer).
- **Profiles**: your personas, each with its own accounts, mods and everything else. Make, rename, recolour, switch
  and delete them here. See [More than one persona (profiles)](../platforms/profiles.md).
- **Audio & text**: pick the **Output devices** (speakers) and **Input devices** (microphones) Cuebot may use. **Culture**
  sets how numbers and dates look, like `1,000.5` or `1.000,5`.
- **Backups** and **Google Drive**: see [Backups](../help/backups.md) and [Backups on Google Drive](../help/google-drive-backups.md).
- **About**: your Cuebot version and where your data folder is. **Open** opens the data folder. It also lists the
  startup switches (see [Command-line options](../help/command-line-options.md)) and every keyboard shortcut.

## On your phone

The phone app, **Cuebot Companion**, is not a page in Cuebot, but it shows a lot of the same things. It has four
tabs: **Deck** (your deck buttons), **Dashboard** (connections, quick actions, subathon timers, goals and top
spenders), **Mods** (changes your mods ask you to approve, and the mod portals you help with) and **Settings**. Pair
it on **Apps > Servers**, under **Pair a phone**. See [The phone app](../mods/phone-app.md).
