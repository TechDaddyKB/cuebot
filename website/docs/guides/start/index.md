# Getting started

New to Cuebot? Start here. These pages get Cuebot onto your computer, signed in and answering your chat, and help you
find your way around.

- [Installing Cuebot](installing.md): download and start Cuebot on Windows, Mac or Linux.
- [Updating Cuebot](updating.md): get a new version, and let Cuebot tell you when one is out.
- [Your first five minutes](first-five-minutes.md): sign in to Twitch, connect OBS and make a `!hello` command.
- [Moving from Streamer.bot](moving-from-streamer-bot.md): bring your Streamer.bot actions across.
- [Every page in Cuebot](every-page.md): a map of every page in the app, with a link to its guide.
- [Words you will see](glossary.md): a small dictionary of the words these guides use.
