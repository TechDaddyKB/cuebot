# Speech, sound and music

This part is about what your stream sounds like. Cuebot can read text out loud, listen to your voice, show the song
you are playing, and take song and video requests from chat. All of it is under **Audio** in the left rail.

## What's in here

- [Text-to-speech](text-to-speech.md): pick a voice and make your actions talk.
- [Reading chat and events aloud](reading-chat-aloud.md): have chat messages, follows, subs and more read out.
- [Voice control](voice-control.md): say a phrase, and Cuebot runs an action.
- [Now playing](now-playing.md): show the song you are playing on stream and in chat.
- [Spotify](spotify.md): connect your Spotify account.
- [Song requests](song-requests.md): let viewers add songs to your Spotify queue with `!sr`.
- [Media requests](media-requests.md): let viewers ask for YouTube videos that play on your stream.
